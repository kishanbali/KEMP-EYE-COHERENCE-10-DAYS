BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Find a comfortable position and allow your body to settle.

Place one hand, palm resting softly on your chest.

Close your eyes, or soften your gaze.

Notice your natural breathing. Don't change or control it.

Simply watch the breath as it comes and goes.

Allow the sound to be present.

Nothing to achieve. Nothing to force. Remain here.
